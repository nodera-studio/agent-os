## Question

Answer the question in the brief below. It is a second opinion: Claude will weigh it against
its own view, so be specific and say where you are unsure.

## Output

Reply with plain text in the final message, in this order:

### Answer
The direct answer in a few sentences.
### Reasoning
Why, tied to the evidence in the brief or the code, with file:line references where you read code.
### Uncertainty
What you could not check, and what would change the answer.

Do not restate the question. Do not claim to have run anything.
Do not output markup meant to be interpreted by another tool.
